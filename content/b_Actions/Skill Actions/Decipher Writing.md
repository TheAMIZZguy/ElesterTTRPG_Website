When you encounter particularly esoteric texts, or one in a language you are not quite proficient enough in (separate translate be different as a language skill, can be done in focus or as an action?), the GM might require you to Decipher the Writing before you can understand it. You must be trained in the relevant skill to Decipher Writing. Aetherology is typically used for magic, science, and religion; History for anything regarding history, people, cities, or political events; Nature for the land and many things untouched by humans; etc. 

### Decipher Writing

#Concentrate  #Exploration #Secret #SkillAction #Trained
**Requirements**: N/A

####
*You attempt to decipher complicated writing or literature on an obscure topic.*

####
This usually takes 1 minute per page of text, but might take longer (typically an hour per page for decrypting ciphers or the like). The text must be in a language you can read, though the GM might allow you to attempt to decipher text written in an unfamiliar language using Society instead. The DC and required proficiency is determined by the GM based on the state or complexity of the document. The GM might have you roll one check for a short text or a check for each section of a larger text. The DC or required proficiency may also increase based on how proficient you are in the language it is written in, or when trying to decipher a single sentence during combat from an action to a quadruple action. 


####
**Critical Success** You understand the true meaning of the text
**Success** You understand the true meaning of the text. If it was a coded document, you know the general meaning but might not have a word-for-word translation
**Failure** You can’t understand the text and take a –2 circumstance penalty to further checks to decipher it.
**Critical Failure** You believe you understand the text on that page, but you have in fact misconstrued its message.


> [!infobox|left]+  
>##### Sample Decipher Tasks  
>**Trained** entry-level philosophy treatise 
>**Skilled** complex code, such as a cipher 
>**Mastered** spymaster’s code or advanced research notes
>**Legendary** enciphered spell notes written in metaphor that are half-missing and contains mistakes
