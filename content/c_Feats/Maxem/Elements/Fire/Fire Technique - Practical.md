*Your fire manifolds become your party’s dependable lever.*

Learn two manifolds from Fire.

You can modify the fire to not emit light or to be any colour.
Additionally, you may light any fuse or small spot of an easily flammable object that is within your Conscious Range for 0MP and at will once per round.
