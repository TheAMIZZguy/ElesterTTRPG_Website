*You understand that the waves are not limited by matter*

When you Evoke a Fire manifold that travels through a solid or liquid medium, the range of that manifold is treated as 2 paces longer than normal.

Additionally, creatures that are submerged in liquid and hit by a Fire manifold through that liquid cannot benefit from any resistance to Fire while submerged.
