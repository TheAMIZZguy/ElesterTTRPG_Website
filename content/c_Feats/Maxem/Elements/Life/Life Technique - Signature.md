*Your life manifolds become your party’s dependable lever.*

Learn two additional Life manifolds.

* When you have over 50% of your Maximum VP, you can sense the number of living beings around you up to 5 paces
* When you have 100% of your Maximum VP, you can sense the general VP (GM discretion) of creatures within 5 paces
