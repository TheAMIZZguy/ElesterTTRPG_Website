*You are no longer merely using earth—you are speaking it as a native.*

Increase your Proficiency within a Material Class.

You may take this feat multiple times.

Additionally, when you Transmute, you may Transmute an adjacent material you know into any other material you know. 
* The volume limit is 5 bits, and the transmutation lasts until the end of the encounter.
