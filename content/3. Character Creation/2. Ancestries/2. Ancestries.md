# Ancestries Overview
 
```ccard
type: folder_brief_live
```
 
Ancestries express the culture your character hails from. Within many ancestries are heritages --subgroups with their characteristics. Ancestries generally define your physique and thus provide your initial Hit Points, Ability Boosts and Flaws, ancestry feats and sometimes more.
