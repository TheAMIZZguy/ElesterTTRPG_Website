# Heritages

##### Black Lung Nascedonian
*So long has your family found themselves in the mines for black iron that it has become enriched in your blood in small amounts*

You gain the Resist Magic Action

![[b_Actions/Cost 1/Resist Magic]]

##### Cliff Runner Nascedonian
*Your ancestors have been travelling the mountains on foot for generations, the adaptations now visible in you*

You become trained in the Agility Skill

##### Rock Foot Nascedonian
*Your ancestors lived and worked among the great ancient stones of the mountains or the depths of the earth. This makes you solid as a rock when you plant your feet*

You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Physical Barrier DC against attempts to Shove or Trip you. This bonus also applies to saving throws against spells or effects that attempt to knock you prone. In addition, if any effect would force you to move 10 feet or more, you are moved only half the distance.
