XP and how to cash it in to get upgrades
- At the moment of writing this I am considering having characters upgrade by "spending XP" on each upgrade
- In terms of understanding what level a character is, there is 1000XP = 1 Level
	- Aside from this general level, every 500(?)XP invested into the Aether, Physical, and Mental(?) Trees determines your level for that thing
	- These sub levels are for balance because if someone is a level 5 in general, most of it having been spent in martial they cant just spend all their remaining XP to go into level 5 Aether weave
		- i.e. you need breadth in an area to accumulate the XP in the area to level it up
- The XP Gained from each Quest/mission/enemy defeated is similar to the pf2e style where it depends on the relative level and difficulty rather than raw amounts
	- A previous identical quest would be worth less XP
