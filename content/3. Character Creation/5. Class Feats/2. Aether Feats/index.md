# Vasilian Rules


Should “Mark” require being tuned to that Field?

I’d do **yes, by default**. It makes the Vasilian’s moment-to-moment choices meaningful: _what are you currently listening to?_ It also prevents “always-on” marking across every Field at no opportunity cost.
- **Default:** You can only **Mark** a creature/thing/phenomenon if you are currently **tuned to the Field** that signature belongs to.
- **Exception space (feat hooks):** Later feats can let you **Mark off-field** (slower, weaker, or with a tell), or let you “snap” a short mark **without retuning** under specific triggers.
- Allow for weaker "unfocused marks" that don't require a Tune
