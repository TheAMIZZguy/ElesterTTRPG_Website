#### 🌟 **Strategic Use**

- Cheap, available, and **resists Blight/Acid poorly**, but has **solid protection vs Impact or Magic weapons**
    
- Druidic or tribal factions can **enhance** this armor type magically
    

#### 💀 **Weaknesses**

- Burns easily (takes 1d4 persistent Fire if critically hit by Fire)
    
- Brittle: heavy strikes may cause chunks to fall off
    
- Swells/softens in humid or soaked conditions unless treated
    

#### 🔁 **Interactions**

- Can be regrown or repaired via **Nature-based magic**
    
- Common in regions where **metal is rare or taboo**
    
- Often targeted by enemies using fire, fungi, or dissolving toxins
    

#### 🔧 **Upgrade Compatibility**

- Can be reinforced with bone, bark, or monster shell
    
- Poor for high-tier enchantments unless inlaid with rare minerals
