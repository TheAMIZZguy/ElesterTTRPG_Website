These are the weapon upgrades that can be applied to a weapon with an open weapon upgrade slot (or replacing one)

If something requires a "hit" or "critical hit" then the aforementioned must happen without the use of the effect
- A "hit" is counted as beating their Physical AC, regardless of any damage reduction that may bring the damage to 0

For the sake of simplicity, ammunition like arrows is considered a "weapon" for the purpose of upgrades
- Example: You can apply the Heavy Head upgrade to a batch of arrows

### How this applies to Ammunition
- Price is reduced to 1/4 (rounded up)
- Applies to a standard batch of the given ammunition type



###### Adaptive Grip
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon with Reach* 
- **Standard**: Receive no circumstance penalty against attacking someone within your Reach


###### Aerodynamic Shape
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon in the Throwing Category*  
- **Standard**: The weapon gets an additional +1 to its item bonus for attack rolls
- **Well-Made**: The weapon gets an additional +2 to its item bonus for attack rolls
- **Master-Craft**: The weapon gets an additional +3 to its item bonus for attack rolls


###### Balanced
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any melee weapon*  
- **Standard**: Reduce the multiple attack penalty for this weapon by 1 on the second attack
- **Well-Made**: Reduce the multiple attack penalty for this weapon by 1 on the second and third attacks
- **Master-Craft**: Reduce the multiple attack penalty for this weapon by 1 on all attacks


###### Barbed Edge
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon with the Grapple trait*  
- **Standard**: Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Grapple attempts using this weapon
- **Well-Made**: Gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Grapple attempts using this weapon
- **Master-Craft**: Gain a +4 circumstance bonus to Grapple attempts using this weapon


###### Breaking Point
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon that deals Bludgeoning damage*  
- **Standard**: When you break a targets armour, deal damage to the target equal to the remaining damage past the BT  
- **Well-Made**: When you break a targets armour, deal damage to the target equal to the damage dealt to the armour


###### Broadhead Tips
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon that deals Piercing damage*  
- **Standard**: Ignore resistance to Piercing damage by 1  
- **Well-Made**: Ignore resistance to Piercing damage by 2
- **Master Craft**: Ignore resistance to Piercing damage by 3


###### Chained Edge
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon in the Sweeping group*  
- **Standard**: On a miss, gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your next attack with this weapon before the end of your turn
- **Well-Made**: On a miss, gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your next attack with this weapon before the end of your turn 
- **Master-Craft**: On a miss, gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your next attack with this weapon before the end of your turn


###### Counterbalance
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon with the Parry Tag*
- **Standard**: Increase the circumstance bonus to your AC by 1 when performing the Parry action
- **Well-Made**: Increase the circumstance bonus to your AC by 2 when performing the Parry action
- **Master-Craft**: Increase the circumstance bonus to your AC by 4 when performing the Parry action


###### Curved Shape
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon
- **Standard**: Ignore the penalty for attacking while Prone  


###### Dual Cutting Edges
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon in the Bladed group*  
- **Standard**: When attacking multiple creatures in the same turn, gain a +1 circumstance bonus to attacks against a creature that was not a target of your previous attack 
- **Well-Made**: When attacking multiple creatures in the same turn, gain a +2 circumstance bonus to attacks against a creature that was not a target of your previous attack 
- **Master Craft**: When attacking multiple creatures in the same turn, gain a +3 circumstance bonus to attacks against a creature that was not a target of your previous attack 


###### Expanding Tip
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon that deals Piercing damage*  
- **Standard**: On a critical hit, target suffers 1d4 persistent bleed damage for 3 rounds.  
- **Well-Made**: On a critical hit, target suffers 2d4 persistent bleed damage for 3 rounds.  
- **Master-Craft**: On a hit, target suffers 3d4 persistent bleed damage for 3 rounds.

###### Flexible Integrity

*Usage: Any weapon in the Flexible Category*

###### Flexible Shaft
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot

- **Well-Made**: Increase the reach of this weapon by 2 units when performing a Trip or Disarm attempt.  


###### Hammer Head
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon in the Impact group*  
- **Standard**: On a critical hit, the target takes a –1 status penalty to AC for 1 round.  
- **Well-Made**: On a critical hit, the target takes a –2 status penalty to AC for 1 round.


###### Heavy Head
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon that deals Piercing or Bludgeoning damage* 
- **Standard**: Increase damage against creatures wearing heavy armor (or equivalent) by 1
- **Well-Made**: Increase damage against creatures wearing heavy armor (or equivalent) by 2
- **Master-Craft**: Increase damage against creatures wearing heavy armor (or equivalent) by 2


###### Heightened Tension
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon in the Ranged Category*  
- **Standard**: The weapons Range increases by 4 units 
- **Well-Made**: The weapons Range increases by half its value (rounded up) (to a minimum increment of 6 units)
- **Master-Craft**: The weapons Range doubles (to a minimum increment of 10 units)


###### Hilt Balance
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon in the Throwing Category*  
- **Standard**: The weapons Range increases by 2 units 
- **Well-Made**: The weapons Range increases by half its value (rounded up) (to a minimum increment of 3 units)
- **Master-Craft**: The weapons Range doubles (to a minimum increment of 5 units)

###### Metal Breaking
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon that deals Piercing or Bludgeoning damage* 
Applies only to targets wearing metal or stone armour
- **Standard**: On a hit, reduce the targets armour HP by the damage dealt  
- **Well-Made**: On a hit, reduce the targets armour HP by the damage dealt. On a critical hit, reduce the targets armour HP by the attack roll result
- **Master-Craft**: On a hit, reduce the targets armour HP by the attack roll result

- **Master-Craft**: If you hit an enemy with this weapon, gain a +3 circumstance bonus to your next attack roll against them this turn


###### Jagged Edge
#WeaponModification  
*Usage: Any weapon *  
- **Standard**: The weapon gains the Deadly trait with a damage die one step lower than its base die (e.g., a d8 weapon gains Deadly d6)  
- **Well-Made**: The weapon gains the Deadly trait with a damage die equal to its base die (e.g., a d12 weapon gains Deadly d12)   
- **Master-Craft**: The weapon gains the Deadly trait with a damage die one step higher than its base die (e.g., a d10 weapon gains Deadly d12)

- **Master-Craft**: When you make a thrown Strike with this weapon to a distance of up to 10 units, you can retrieve it as part of an interaction without needing to move
