Any weapon with Black Iron is able to use the tiers below it
A weapon with Black Iron cannot use a crystal upgrade



###### Black Iron Dusting
#WeaponUpgrade #BlackIron 
- **Passive**: While wielding this weapon, gain a +1 item bonus to your Aetherical AC against magical attacks.

###### Black Iron Painting
#WeaponUpgrade #BlackIron 
 **Passive**: On a critical hit, if the target uses an aetherical ability, they take 1d6 Psychic damage

###### Black Iron Coating
#WeaponUpgrade #BlackIron 
**Activate** \[◆] 
**Trigger** You get hit by an aetherical effect
**Effect** Make an attack roll with the weapon, reduce damage taken by the roll rather than your AC

###### Black Iron Alloy
#WeaponUpgrade #BlackIron 
**Activate** \[◆◆] Strike  
**Effect** On a hit, the target loses AP equal to the damage taken

###### Pure Black Iron
#WeaponUpgrade #BlackIron 
**Activate** \[◆] Strike
**Effect** On a hit, the target cannot cast or activate Aetherical abilities until the start of your next turn
