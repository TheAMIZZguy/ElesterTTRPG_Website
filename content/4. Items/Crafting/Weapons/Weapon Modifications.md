Weapons can have up to 1 modifications

These are situational or minor improvements
- Such as giving/removing a trait from a weapon (like giving tripping or removing heavy)
- Or improving some situational aspect like +5 to hiding the weapon from people searching for it

Not every modification can apply to every weapon, but most do

Stronger/More useful weapon modifications would be classified as a weapon upgrade



###### Belt Hook
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon that can be sheathed*  
- **Standard**: The weapon can be drawn or stowed as part of a Stride action 

###### Blunt-Tipped
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon that deals Piercing damage*  
- **Standard**: The weapon can deals Bludgeoning damage instead of Piercing damage

###### Blunted Edge
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon that deals Slashing or Piercing damage*  
- **Standard**: The weapon gains the Nonlethal trait.  

###### Camouflaged
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon*  
- **Standard**: You gain a +1 item bonus to Slyness checks and DCs to hide or conceal this weapon
- **Well-Made**: You gain a +2 item bonus to Slyness checks and DCs to hide or conceal this weapon
- **Master-Craft**: You gain a +3 item bonus to Slyness checks and DCs to hide or conceal this weapon

###### Dense
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon
- **Standard**: The weapon requires a Strength of +3 to use


###### Fortified Shaft
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon with Reach*  
- **Standard**: The weapon gains the Shove Trait 

###### Folded Crossguard
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any bladed weapon*  
- **Standard**: Makes the weapon easier to store and transport, reducing its Bulk by 1 (to a minimum of 1).  

###### Grooved Handle
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon with a grip*  
- **Standard**: You do not need to drop the weapon on a critical failure when attempting to Trip or Disarm   

###### Hefty
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification   
*Usage: Any weapon with Reach* 
- **Standard**: Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to resist Shove attempts
- **Well-Made**: Gain a +2 circumstance bonus to resist Shove attempts
- **Master-Craft**: Gain a +4 circumstance bonus to resist Shove attempts

###### Improved Grip
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any melee weapon*  
- **Standard**: Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to resist being Disarmed.  
- **Well-Made**: Gain a +2 circumstance bonus to resist being Disarmed.  
- **Master-Craft**: Gain a +4 circumstance bonus to resist being Disarmed.  

###### Keen
#WeaponUpgrade #UpgradeSlot  
*Elements: Any Weapon*  
- **Standard**: Attacks with this weapon explode on a 1 on the attack dice rather than on the max roll

###### Precision Hilt
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon in the Bladed or Thrusting group*  
- **Standard**: The weapon gains the Parry trait

###### Resistant Material
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon*  
- **Standard**: Mending the weapon also Repairs it

###### Reversible Grip
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon that deals slashing damage*  
- **Standard**: The weapon gains the Sweep trait

###### Sharp Point
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon that deals Bludgeoning damage*  
- **Standard**: The weapon can deals Piercing damage instead of Bludgeoning damage

###### Shortened Grip
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any two-handed melee weapon*  
- **Standard**: The weapon is able to be used with one hand, decreasing its weapon damage die by two when wielded with one hand  
- **Well-Made**: The weapon is able to be used with one hand, decreasing its weapon damage die by one when wielded with one hand  

###### Skew Shape
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any melee weapon that deals Slashing or Piercing damage*  
- **Standard**: The weapon gains the Trip trait.  

###### Thick Guard
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any weapon in the Shield category*  
- **Standard**: Increase this weapon’s hardness by 1.  
- **Well-Made**: Increase this weapon’s hardness by 2.  
- **Master-Craft**: Increase this weapon’s hardness by 2.  

###### Weighted End
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Any melee weapon*  
- **Standard**: The weapon gains the Backswing trait

###### Wide Head
#WeaponUpgrade #Modification  
*Usage: Ammunition  
- **Standard**: The weapon gains the Trip trait
