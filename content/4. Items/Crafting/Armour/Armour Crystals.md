### Crystals


###### Fire Crystals
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot  
_Usage: Torso_
- **Dusting**: You can spend 1 HP to gain +1 Pace to your movement speed
- **Studding**: Once per round you can spend 5 HP to gain +1 AP (to a maximum of 4)
- **Shards**: Aether with a cost of at least 3 AP is 1 MP cheaper, but costs an additional 1 HP to cast


###### Ice Crystals
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot  
_Usage: Torso_
- **Dusting**: -1 DC to respond to the action of any creature within 4 paces of you
- **Studding**: -1 DC for anyone to respond to the action of any creature within 4 paces of you
- **Shards**: Can use 1 MP to pay for the cost of 1 SP

###### Death Crystals 
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot  
_Usage: Torso_
- **Dusting**: A creature who makes an action to attack you must pass a DC 3 flat check to hit you
- **Studding**: Heal for 1 HP for every 5 damage dealt to a creature within 4 paces. To a maximum of 25 HP total.
- **Shards**: -1 item penalty to perception and speed to all creatures within 4 units. 


###### Earth Crystals
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot

- **Studding**: +1 item bonus to Strength, -1 item bonus to Presence
- **Shards**: -1 pace to any forced movement against you, the armour sinks in water

###### Static Crystals
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot  
_Usage: Torso_
