These are the armour upgrades that can be applied to armour with an open armour upgrade slot (or replacing one)

A "hit" is counted as beating any relevant Defense, regardless of any damage reduction that may bring the damage to 0



###### Angled Plates
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any Composite or Steel Armour  
- **Standard**: The armour gains Entrench (Ranged) 
- **Well-Made**: The armour upgrades Entrench (Ranged) so the bonus is +2
- **Master-Craft**: The armour upgrades Entrench (Ranged) so the bonus is +3


###### Archery Bracers
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Arm*  
- **Well-Made**: Critical failures become failures when making a ranged attack


###### Add-On Spurs
#WeaponModification  
*Usage: Leg*  
- **Standard**: +1 item bonus to riding  
- **Well-Made**: +2 item bonus to riding  


###### Balanced Greaves
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Leg*  
- **Standard**: Reduce Movement penalties from the environment by 1 pace
- **Well-Made**: Reduce Movement penalties from the environment by 2 paces
- **Master-Craft**: Reduce Movement penalties from the environment by 3 paces


###### Barbed Edge
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any Medium Armour
- **Standard**: Deal 1 piercing damage to an attacker that makes a critical fail melee attack towards you
- **Well-Made**: Deal 2 piercing damage to an attacker that makes a critical fail melee attack towards you
- **Master-Craft**: Deal 4 piercing damage to an attacker that makes a critical fail melee attack towards you


###### Counterweights
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Heavy or Advanced* 
- **Standard**: Removes the Hindering Property


###### Doubled
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any  
- **Master-Craft**: +2 Item bonus to Physical Defense, but the armour gains +1 Bulk


###### Extra Material
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any*  
- **Standard**: Increase armour HP by 5
- **Well-Made**: Increase armour HP by 10
- **Master-Craft**: Increase armour HP by 20


###### Fur Lining
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Cloth*  
- **Standard**: Armour gains Insulated Cold


###### Gilded Fronting
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Torso*  
- **Standard**: The armour gains the civilized trait 
- **Well-Made**: The armour gains the civilized trait and +1 item bonus to social checks
- **Master Craft**: The armour gains the civilized trait and +3 item bonus to social checks


###### Gripped Sides
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Arm*
- **Standard**: +1 item bonus to making actions tagged with disarm
- **Well-Made**: +2 item bonus to making actions tagged with disarm


###### Hooked Rings
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any armour*
- **Standard**: You can attach and retrieve an item on your back as 1 AP rather than 2 AP (double check this action cost on retrieve and store) 


###### Intimidating Decoration
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Torso, Head*  
- **Standard**: +1 item bonus to intimidation checks while wearing this armour
- **Well-Made**: +2 item bonus to intimidation checks while wearing this armour
- **Master Craft**: +3 item bonus to intimidation checks while wearing this armour


###### Lightweight
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Medium, Heavy, Advanced*  
- **Standard**: Reduce bulk by 1 (to a minimum of 1)
- **Well-Made**: Reduce bulk by 1 (to a minimum of L)
- **Master-Craft**: Reduce bulk by 2 (to a minimum of 1)


###### Monster Trophy Rigged
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Composite*  
- **Standard**: Gain +1 item bonus on mental attacks if your armour is made of the same material as the enemy you are targeting  
- **Well-Made**: Gain +2 item bonus on mental attacks if your armour is made of the same material as the enemy you are targeting  
- **Master-Craft**: Gain +3 item bonus on mental attacks if your armour is made of the same material as the enemy you are targeting   


###### Natural Allure
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Wood* 
- **Standard**: +1 item bonus to charisma checks on any creature tagged with Nature
- **Well-Made**: +2 item bonus to charisma checks on any creature tagged with Nature
- **Master-Craft**: +3 item bonus to charisma checks on any creature tagged with Nature


######  Odd-Edges
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any weapon in the Advanced*  
- **Standard**: +1 item bonus against being grappled by a weapon


###### Oil Coating
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any armour not in the cloth group*  
Adds Flammable tag to the armour
- **Standard**: Get a +2 circumstance bonus against being grappled
- **Well-Made**: Get a +3 circumstance bonus against being grappled
- **Master-Craft**: Get a +4 circumstance bonus against being grappled


###### Padded Coif
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Head*  
- **Standard**: +1 additional item bonus to PD vs Bludgeoning and gets Laminar


###### Plated Collar  
_Usage: Torso, Head_
- **Standard**: Grants the Bulwark tag


###### Polished
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Metal*  
- **Well-Made**: Gain the Polished Tag


###### Protective Coating
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any*  
- **Standard**: Armour gains resistance 2 against acid damage
- **Well-Made**: Armour gains resistance 4 against acid damage
- **Master Craft**: Armour gains resistance 6 against acid damage


###### Quick-Release Lacing
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Light*
- **Well-Made**: You may doff the armour with 4 AP
- **Master-Craft**: You may doff the armour with 2 AP and don with 4 AP


###### Quality Materials
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any* 
- **Standard**: Increase hardness by 1
- **Well-Made**: Increase hardness by 2
- **Master-Craft**: Increase hardness by 4


###### Reinforced Plate
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Steel*
- **Standard**: Deal 2 flat damage to the object that makes a critical fail melee attack towards you
- **Well-Made**: Deal 4 piercing damage to the object that makes a critical fail melee attack towards you
- **Master-Craft**: Deal 6 piercing damage to the object that makes a critical fail melee attack towards you


###### Smoothed Links
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Light*  
- **Standard**:  You may spend 1 AP to 
	- Reduce your armours item bonus by 1
	- But removing its speed penalty until the end of your next turn 


###### Split Seams
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Wood*  
- **Standard**: Any attack that hits by 2 damage or less is stuck to the armour as seen in the tag Sticky
- **Well-Made**: Any attack that hits by 4 damage or less is stuck to the armour as seen in the tag Sticky
- **Master-Craft**: Any attack that hits by 6 damage or less is stuck to the armour as seen in the tag Sticky


###### Tailor-Made
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Light* 
- **Standard**: +1 item bonus to agility checks


###### Thickened
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Any*  
- **Standard**: Armour gains resistance 1 against physical damage
- **Well-Made**: Armour gains resistance 2 against physical damage


###### Weighted Legs
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Legs*
- **Standard**: Get a +2 circumstance bonus against being tripped
- **Well-Made**: Get a +4 circumstance bonus against being tripped
- **Master Craft**: Get a +6 circumstance bonus against being tripped


###### Whisper Hood
#ArmourUpgrade #UpgradeSlot 
*Usage: Cloth*  
- **Standard**: Gain the Silent Tag
