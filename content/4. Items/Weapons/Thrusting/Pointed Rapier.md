## Critical Effect
Choose one creature adjacent to the initial target and within reach. If its Physical Defence is lower than your attack roll result for the critical hit, you deal damage to that creature equal to the surplus in the hit to AC. This amount cannot allow for another [[Weapons#Critical Specialization Effect|Critical Specialization Effect]], and no bonuses or other additional dice apply to this damage.

###### Fatal
