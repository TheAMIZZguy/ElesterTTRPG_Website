## Description
*This bow is taller than most men and usually made of a single piece of elm, hickory, or yew, has a powerful draw and is excellent at propelling arrows with great force and at an extreme distance. You must use two hands to fire a longbow, and it can't be used while mounted.*

## Critical Effect
If the target of the attack is adjacent to a surface it gets stuck to that surface by the missile. The targets is [[Immobilized]] and must spend an Interact action to attempt a DC 10 Fortitude check to pull the missile free. It cannot move the its space until it succeeds. If the creature cannot become stuck, it gains [[Stunned|Stunned 1]] instead.



## Traits
 
###### Deadly
- On a critical hit, the weapon rolls an additional die of the same amount

###### Volley
- *This ranged weapon is less effective at close distances*
- Your attacks against targets that are at a distance within the range listed take a –2 circumstance penalty
