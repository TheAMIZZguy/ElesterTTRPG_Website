## Description
*These immense bows are traditionally made from the horns of great beasts, though modern residents of the wastelands often use composite materials or even small trees as the weapon's base. While horsebows have a shorter range than other bows, they make up for it by focusing the power of the longbow at a short distance and can be used from horseback—perfect for the skirmishing fighting style of mounted wastelander tribes.*

## Critical Effect
If the target of the attack is adjacent to a surface it gets stuck to that surface by the missile. The targets is [[Immobilized]] and must spend an Interact action to attempt a DC 10 Fortitude check to pull the missile free. It cannot move the its space until it succeeds. If the creature cannot become stuck, it gains [[Stunned|Stunned 1]] instead.
