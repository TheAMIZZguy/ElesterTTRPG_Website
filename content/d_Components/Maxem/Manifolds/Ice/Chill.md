## Effects
*The staple of ice, a simple freeze*

**Effects:** Freeze an object 
- The object is frozen if your attack roll + 1d4 beats its BT
- A creature is frozen if your attack roll + 1d4 beats its AD
- Creatures and objects frozen by this take 1d4 persistent Cold damage. 


## Weave Notes

- *Direct* will only effect the targeted object
- Will only effect the targeted object



## Other Notes

- Can only be cast through air
- You can choose to cool instead of freeze even without a max roll
- A frozen object gains a +1 status bonus against being ignited
