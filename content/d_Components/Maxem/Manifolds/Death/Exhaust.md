## Effects
*Hook a loose thread of awareness and reel in a single soft echo from a living mind*

**Effects:** Make an Aetherics attack vs Aetherical Defense of a living creature 
- **Critical Failure:** You cannot spend a Whisper on this person for 1 round
- **Failure:** You harvest 0 Whisper
- **Success:** You harvest 1 Whisper
- **Critical Success:** You harvest 2 Whisper

**Max held:**  1
While you hold a Whisper, for 1 AP you can spend up to your PRE mod (min 1) to create 1 minor weakness for a number of rounds equal to the number of whisper you spent
