## Effects
*What is free will but a manifestation of the consciousness of thought*

**Effects:** You suggest a course of action to the target, which must be phrased in such a way as to seem like a logical course of action to the target and can't be self-destructive or obviously against the target's self-interest

- **Critical Failure:** No effect; target knows you tried to influence them
- **Failure:** No effect
- **Success:** Effects of your success tier
- **Critical Success:** Effects of your success tier+1

Tiers:
- **Success 1:** The target listens to a command of 1 word
- **Success 2:** The target listens to a simple short command
- **Success 3:** The target listens to a simple sentence
- **Success 4:** The target listens to a complex sentence


## Weave Notes

* Heighten: The base success tier increases by 1 (starts at 1 when not heightened)
