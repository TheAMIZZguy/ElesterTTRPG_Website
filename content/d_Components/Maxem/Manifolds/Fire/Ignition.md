## Effects
*The staple of Fire, a simple burn*

**Effects:** Light a flammable object on fire.
- The object is lit if your attack roll + 1d4 beats its BT
- A creature is lit if your attack roll + 1d4 beats its AD
- Creatures and objects burnt by this fire take 1d4 persistent Fire damage.
