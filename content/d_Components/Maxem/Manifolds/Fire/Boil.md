## Effects
*Energy travelling through something as cooling and moving as water needs a specialized technique*

**Effects:** Boil an object in a liquid in a heat that stifles energy loss
- The object is boiled if your attack roll + 1d4 beats its BT
- A creature is boiled if your attack roll + 1d4 beats its AD
- Creatures and objects boiled by this fire take 1d4 persistent Fire damage.
