## Effects
*Matter ceases to exist*

**Effects:** One of
- Deal 1d4 blight damage to an object of a given Material Class
	- If an item reaches its breaking threshold from this it is destroyed
- Destroy an area of a pure material class
	- Either a disk, sphere, rectangular plank, ingot of total volume known as 1 bit (about 27 cubic centimeters, or a cube of 1.2in side length)
	- The entire object must be of a single material class, and it must be of a material class you know.


## Weave Notes

- **Area**: 1 bit -> 5 bits -> 25 bits -> 100 bits -> 1000 bits -> 2000bits -> 5000 bits


## Other Notes

- Must be learned for each Material Class
