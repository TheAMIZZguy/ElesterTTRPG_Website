## Effects
*Changing the flows of the world*

**Effects:** You can redirect currents 
- The DC of the score to move against it is equal to your PRE + CON + 0d4 (min 1) 
- The maximum Bulk of an item that it can push away is equal to the DC / 4 (round up)

## Weave Notes

- **Heighten**: Sizes start (in volume) from hand (default) -> pace -> 5 paces -> 25 paces -> 100 paces


## Other Notes

- It can be air current, water currents, magma currents, etc. Current means any flow of liquid/gas
- This can cause drifts in gaseous materials and particulate
- This can induce conditions like [[Immobilized]] and [[Prone]]
