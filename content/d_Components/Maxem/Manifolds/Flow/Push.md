## Effects
*An object in motion*

**Effects:** You can move solid material of the given size through 1 pace of uninterrupted space
- If there is something on top or inside of the object being moved it moves alongside it so long as it has a bulk of L or lower
- If it hits someone (against their PD) this deals 1d4 damage of the type that makes the most sense (usually bludgeoning)


## Weave Notes

- **Heighten**: Sizes start (in volume) from hand (default) -> pace -> 5 paces -> 25 paces -> 100 paces
	- This also goes from L -> 1 Bulk -> 3 Bulk -> 9 Bulk -> 30 Bulk -> 100 Bulk (?)
- **Enhanced**: The weight tier goes up by 1 of what it can carry 
- **Augment**: The weight tier goes up by 1 of what it can carry 


## Other Notes

- Moving something attached to someone (notably armour/clothing) a roll to beat their AD is required
- This can also be used to hold an object in place (by "moving" it to where it already is)
- This can induce conditions like [[Immobilized]] and [[Prone]]
