## Effects
*What you see might not be real*

**Effects:** You create an illusory visual image of a stationary object or creature
- The entire image must fit within the spell's area.
- The object appears to animate naturally, but it doesn't make sounds or generate smells. 
	- For example, water would appear to pour down an illusory waterfall, but it would be silent.
- Any creature that touches the image or uses the Seek action (TODO) to examine it can attempt to disbelieve your illusion.
- You can use an action to have the illusion move or "perform an action" when you Sustain the spell
- The illusion can cause HP damage by making the target believe the illusion's attacks are real, but it cannot otherwise directly affect the physical world nor cause VP damage. 
	- If the illusory creature hits with a Strike, the target takes 1d4 psychic damage.
- If the damage doesn't correspond to the image of the monster the GM might allow the target to attempt an immediate Perception check to disbelieve the spell
	- for example, if an illusory Large dragon that dealt only 5 damage or a severe attack that didn't seem to cause major damage
- When a creature disbelieves the illusion, it recovers from half the damage it had taken from it (if any) and doesn't take any further damage from it.


## Weave Notes

- **Area**: Sizes start (in volume) from hand (default) -> pace -> 5 paces -> 25 paces -> 100 paces


## Other Notes


- If the image is hit by an attack you need to use a reaction to make the hit look convincing but it does NOT disappear
