## Effects
*A bane to those who use forged items.*

**Effects:** Makes something magnetic
- magnetic objects are attracted to it by a force of 1d4 paces per round
	- This may cause bludgeoning or piercing damage as seen fit
- Moving an magnetic item away from it requires a STR + 1d4 roll against your fixed PRE + 1d4 to succeed
- Detaching an magnetic item away from it requires PRE + CON + 1d4 to succeed


## Weave Notes

- **Area**: how much the magnetic field reaches



## Other Notes

- This can induce conditions like [[Immobilized]] and [[Prone]] 
- If the item is diamagnetic (i.e. wood) then it receives a -4 to the DCs
