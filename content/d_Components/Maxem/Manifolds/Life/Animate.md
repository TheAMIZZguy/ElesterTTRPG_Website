## Effects
*A statue is brought to life*

**Effects:** Choose 1 small object within range that is an effigy or statue of something that could move
- Each being animated this way is under your control until the evocation ends or until it is reduced to 0 VP
- For 1 AP you can Command the being so long as it is within 12 paces
	- This Command includes what it will do with all its AP that turn, if it should prepare a reaction, or a simple general instruction such as "guard the corridor"
	- If no commands have been given then it will only defend itself against hostile creatures
	- It will follow its order until the task is complete
	- If you control multiple creatures, then you can command all of them with the same Command but at 2 AP
