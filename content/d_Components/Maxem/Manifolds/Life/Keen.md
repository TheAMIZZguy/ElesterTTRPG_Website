## Effects
*Spur the mind into keen alertness*

**Effects:**  Gain +1 status bonus to any one of WIT, WIL, or RES but then gain -1 status penalty to the same stat for the same duration after it ends
- If it is not a willing creature, then this applies only on a 2d4 aetherical attack against their AD


## Weave Notes

-  **Heighten**: Increases the status bonus but not penalty


## Other Notes

- Does not affect mindless entities
- The default duration of instant means implies that it is must be used *duration* casting (such as with a reaction)
