## Effects
*Creation*

When you touch or focus on an object of a Material Class you know, you my attempt a secret Aetherics roll against its Hardness or the AD of the evoker.
* **Critical Success**: you also learn its approximate age and origin material
* **Success**: you learn its composition, purity, and whether it has been recently transmuted or artificially treated
* **Failure**: you learn incorrect information that is close to correct
* **Critical Failure**: you learn incorrect information that is wildly incorrect but sounds plausible
