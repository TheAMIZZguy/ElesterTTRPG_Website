## Effects
*Creation*

**Effects:** Create a small item of the material
- Either a disk, sphere, rectangular plank, ingot of total volume known as 1 bit (about 27 cubic centimeters, or a cube of 1.2in side length)


## Weave Notes

- **Area**: 1 bit -> 5 bits -> 25 bits -> 100bits -> 1000 bits -> 2000bits -> 5000 bits
- **Duration**: Cost is reduced by 1 MP per tier



## Other Notes

- Must be learned for each Material Class
- ~~Not available to the Metal and Rocky Class (Undiscovered manifolds)~~
- Organic matter created this way is not alive unless it is [[Vitalize|Vitalized]] or was grown as an extension of a living being
- After the length of creation, the matter begins to disintegrate at a rate such that it is entirely gone by twice the creation time
