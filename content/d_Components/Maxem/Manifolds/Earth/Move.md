## Effects
*The staple of Earth, the will of an object to be elsewhere*

**Effects:** You can move material of the given size through 1 pace of uninterrupted space
- If there is something on top or inside of the object being moved it moves alongside it so long as it has a bulk of L or lower
- If it hits someone (against their PD) this deals 1d4 damage of the type that makes the most sense (usually bludgeoning)


## Weave Notes

- **Heighten**: Sizes start (in volume) from hand (default) -> pace -> 5 paces -> 25 paces -> 100 paces
- **Enhanced**: The weight tier goes up by 1 of what it can carry 
- **Augment**: increase the total number of object areas you can control at once
	- I.e. With a weight tier of 1, you can control two sets of objects, the summed weight being less than or equal to 1



## Other Notes

- Note that the distance includes vertical movement, so making something go up 1 pace and then to the side 1 pace is 2 paces of range
- Must be learned for each Material Class
- Available to all Material Classes
- Moving something attached to someone (notably armour/clothing) a roll to beat their AD is required
