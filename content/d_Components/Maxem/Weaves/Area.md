Upon reaching it's destination, its extends out onto a shape, dousing the effect onto everything within it
- Sphere: Radius of 2 paces
- Cylinder: Radius of 2 paces, height of 4
- Line: A continuous line of 5 paces
- Cone: A radius of 4 paces up to 45 degrees from the center


## Notes

- For simplicity, the radius counts after the center tile
	- I.e. A sphere of radius 4 includes the center tile plus 4 paces in each direction
	- Radius 0 would still require the use of area to cover the tile

- This weave can be repeated multiple times to duplicate the effects
	- I.e. Explosive Area 3 can make a sphere of radius 6, with an MP cost of 6 and level 3
