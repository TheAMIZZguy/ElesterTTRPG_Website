The range of this attack is equal to 4 paces


## Notes

- You must have line of sight of the trajectory of the beam or otherwise the target is Concealed in terms of the attack
	- The failed flat check still consumes the spell, but may hit something else at GM discretion\
- The spell trajectory may be blocked by a physical medium (like a wall or a lot of water) or a wall of the same field
	- The total length of the path it takes must still be less than or equal to the range

- This weave can be applied multiple times to stack the effects
	- I.e. Range 3 makes the range 4x3=12 paces at a cost of 3 MP and level 0.75
