Instead of immediately evoking a manifold, after paying the necessary costs, you can pay 1 MP to hold the manifold in place to activate on a specific trigger


## Notes

- The held manifold is visible in the fields 
- The manifold cannot be changed it is set
- The trigger is held for up to one year if nothing disturbs the surrounding related field
- You can evoke other manifolds even if the trigger effect hasn't activated yet
- A failed counter-evoke on a resting trigger will still cause the trigger
