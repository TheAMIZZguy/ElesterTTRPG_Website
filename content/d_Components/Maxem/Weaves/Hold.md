Instead of immediately evoking a manifold, after paying the necessary costs, you can pay 1 MP to hold the manifold for a minute,


## Notes

- If the manifold reaches the 1 minute mark, you can choose to pay 1MP to hold it for another minute
	- Otherwise the spell will fade out 
- You cannot evoke a different spell
