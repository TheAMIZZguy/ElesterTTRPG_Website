*You pick up the thread that ties a thing to what it’s about to become.*

---
Choose a creature, object, or defined area (e.g., “the doorway”, “the wagon”). You gain a **Mark Thread** on it. The target is **Marked (by you)**.

A Mark ends when:
- you **Release** it, or
- the encounter ends, or
- you have had **no line of sight** to the target for **1 minute**.
