On **Success**: You gain an **Analysis** on that target until the end of the encounter.

While you have Analysis on a target:
- Your **first Vasilian Exploit each round** against that target gains **+1**.

On **Critical Success (+10)**: You also learn one meaningful weakness/instability (GM: resistance/vulnerability, bad footing, overcommit tendency, etc.).

On **Failure**: No effect.

**Scaling**
- **Mastered**: Maintain Analysis on **two** targets.
