*You notice who is steering the room without speaking.*

---
On **Success**: You learn the group’s current “center” (fear/greed/anger/duty/curiosity/etc.) and one “pressure point” (what they most want avoided or achieved).

On **Critical Success (+10)**: You also identify who holds the social leverage.

On **Failure**: No effect.

**Scaling**
- **Mastered**: You can keep this read as a Thread (counts toward Strain).
- **Legendary**: If kept as a Thread, it does not count toward Strain.
