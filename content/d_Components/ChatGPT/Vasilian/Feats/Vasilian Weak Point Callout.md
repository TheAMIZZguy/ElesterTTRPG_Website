*You name the crack so everyone stops hitting armor.*

---
Choose one of the target’s defenses: **PD, MD, or AD**.

On **Success**: Until the start of your next turn, the target treats that Defense as **1 lower** against the **first** roll made by each ally (max **2** allies can benefit).

On **Critical Success (+10)**: Up to **3** allies can benefit.

On **Failure**: No effect.

**Scaling**
- **Mastered**: Up to **3** allies can benefit on success.
- **Legendary**: The chosen Defense counts **2 lower** for the first beneficiary each round.
