*You point at the exact moment where reality slips.*

---
On **Success**: Choose one ally who can perceive you. Until the start of your next turn, that ally gains **+1** on their **next roll** that targets the Marked thing (attack, contest, or skill check—whatever is relevant).

On **Critical Success (+10)**: The ally also treats the target’s relevant Defense as **1 lower** for that roll.

On **Failure**: No effect.

On **Critical Failure (−10)**: Your chosen ally instead takes **−1** on that roll.

**Scaling by Vasilian Mastery**
- **Skilled**: Range is 60 paces.
- **Mastered**: Choose **two** allies instead of one.
- **Legendary**: Bonus becomes **+2**; critical success becomes “Defense **2 lower** for that roll.”
