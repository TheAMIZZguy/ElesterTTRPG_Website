*Add a rider to your harm essences that messes with movement or focus.*

---
Once per round when one of your harm essences takes effect on a creature for the first time, add one rider (GM may restrict by formula):
- **Slowed 1** until end of its next turn, OR
- **-1** to mental actions until end of its next turn, OR
- **Sickened 1** for 1 round (or equivalent).
