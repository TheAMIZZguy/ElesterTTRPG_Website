*Your WIL keeps you steady when a brew starts to go wrong.*

---
When you suffer a **Mishap**, roll the Mishap die with advantage:
- roll **2d6**, keep the **lower** result for the “higher than WIL” gate.

Once per Rest, you may ignore the **Stunned 1** outcome from a Mishap (you still resolve the rest).
