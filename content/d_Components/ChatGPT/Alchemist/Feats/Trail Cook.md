*Turn cheap supplies into morale/comfort that matter over long travel.*

---
During a Rest, prepare one meal for up to 4 creatures. Choose one benefit until next Rest:
- reduce cold discomfort penalties by 1 step
- reduce hunger penalties by 1 step
- +1 to one non-combat skill check during travel (first use only)

Rule: only **one meal and one drink** benefit at a time.
