*A last-ditch intervention when a party member would fold.*

---
Once per Rest, when an essence effect would apply a severe outcome to a creature in Touch (GM discretion), you may:
- immediately apply a Stabilizer/Purifier essence you brewed, and
- treat it as **Potent** for the purpose of reducing condition tiers.
