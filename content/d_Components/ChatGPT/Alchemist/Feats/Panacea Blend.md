*Combine remedies so one dose covers multiple problems (at a cost).*

---
When brewing a healer essence in downtime, you may add one secondary rider:
- Antitoxin rider (+2 vs toxin-like effects for 10 min)
- Antiplague rider (+2 vs disease-like effects for 10 min)
- Wakefulness rider (ignore sleep pressure penalties for 10 min)

The GM may require extra reagents or a named reagent for the added rider.
