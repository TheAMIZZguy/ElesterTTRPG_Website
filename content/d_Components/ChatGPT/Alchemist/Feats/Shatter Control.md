*Shape spread to avoid allies or punish clustering.*

---
Once per round when you deploy a vial/dust you brewed, choose one:
- **Selective Spread**: up to 2 creatures in the affected area take no secondary exposure (GM adjudicates), OR
- **Brutal Spread**: increase the area’s intensity slightly (GM).

If Brutal Spread is disallowed for that essence, instead gain **+1** to your first WIT vs Defense roll for that essence.
