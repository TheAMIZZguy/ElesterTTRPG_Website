*Know more formulas than anyone expects, and recall them under pressure.*

---
You learn **2 additional Tier-1** formulas.

Once per scene, you may declare you “remember” a plausible formula you had previously encountered in play.
The GM either allows you to add it to your Formula Book immediately, or asks for a short justification / KNO check.
