*Your purifiers can neutralize unknown residue and mixed brews (limited).*

---
When you use a Purifier essence you brewed, you may attempt to cleanse an **unknown** essence effect.

Make a Brew Check vs the unknown essence’s Tier (GM sets Tier if unknown):
- **Success**: suppress the effect for 1 round (combat) or 10 minutes (downtime)
- **Critical Success**: remove it entirely (GM may restrict if plot-critical)
- **Failure**: no effect
- **Critical Failure**: your purifier becomes inert (wasted)

Limit: once per scene.
