## Tune: Field of Force (Flow / Static)

**Default Range**: 5 paces

**Tuning I** 
While tuned to the Field of Force, you cannot be truly disoriented by motion.
- You never lose your sense of direction.
- You always know verticality (up/down), even in darkness, falling, spinning, or zero reference.
- You always know your relative motion (whether you are moving, what direction, and whether you are accelerating/decelerating).

**Tuning II** 
You can track the movement of every object within your range.
- Such as a creature moving around or even grabbing something behind there back
- But no details other than knowing movement happened

**Tuning III** 
All your senses become [[Precise Sense|precise]] senses
