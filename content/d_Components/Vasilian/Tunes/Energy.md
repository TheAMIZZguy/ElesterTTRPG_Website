## Tune: Field of Energy (Fire / Ice)

**Default Range**: 5 paces

**Tuning I** 
While tuned to the Field of Energy, you sense recent excitations left by evocation.
- You know if a Manifold was evoked within range recently.
- You can tell roughly how long ago it occurred.

**Tuning II:**
You can tell if a Manifold is being actively evoked around you.
- Before it takes into effect.
- You do not know from where.
- You do not know what is being evoked. 

**Tuning III** 
You can tell if a Manifold is being actively evoked around you.
- Before it takes into effect.
- You can point out the general direction.
- You identify the Element being used.
