## Tune: Field of Thought (Life / Death)

**Default Range**: 5 paces

**Tune I**
While tuned to the Field of Thought, you are constantly aware of living signatures.
- You always know the number of living creatures.
	- With a high enough level of consciousness
- You can tell whether a creature within range is alive, dying, or sustained unnaturally.

**Tuning II** 
When a creature within range takes an action, you can tell if that action was intentional, compelled, or reflexive.

**Tuning III**
Your read becomes sharper: you can tell whether a creature is more friendly or more hostile towards you.
