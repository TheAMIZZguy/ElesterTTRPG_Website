## Tune: Field of Existence (Earth / Blight)

**Default Range**: 5 paces

**Tuning I** 
While tuned to the Field of Existence, you read the “honesty” of matter and structure.
- You ignore difficult terrain penalties caused by structure (rubble, broken flooring, loose stone, cramped framing), but not by hazards (slick ice, caltrops, acid pools, active manifold effects, etc.).
- You always know which spaces within range are load-bearing.
- You always know where the footing is weakest (rot, hollowing, loose substrate, compromised stone).

**Tuning II**
You can distinguish natural land from tampering (recent digging, deliberate sabotage, hidden voids).
- Note: a trap in the forest may be clear, but a trap in the dungeon will be generally surrounded by tampered land and so not be noticeable

**Tuning III** 
You can “feel” stress in the structure.
- You know the HP and Breaking Threshold of items around you are. 
- You know which nearby support point is closest to failure if it takes more weight or impact.
