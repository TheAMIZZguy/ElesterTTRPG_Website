Notes:
- Fun roleplay for things that are more chill
- But can completely ruin a character concept
	- Okay sometimes if the character can retire, but not always
	- Re-spec can only be done to an extent
- A simple "critical hit" should not be just enough, base it on % of Max HP (50%?)
- Potentially Light, Moderate, and Heavy wounds 
	- Could have separate HP requirement each: 25%, 50%, 75%?
	- And VP: 1, 2, 3?
- Try to sneak in potential boots (the injury is a downside but it can come with a boon)
	- Such as a scar over the eye giving +Persuasion (Intimidation)
	- Or a prosthetic arm may make it so you must attack an item to it (takes a while and before any combat occurs), but you now can't be disarmed from it
	- The goal is to mix this with character growth and roleplay. An injury SHOULD be impactful, but the play may not like being a weaker character than party
- Player input on what injury they get may even be possible to an extent (roll 3 times and they can choose from any of the three)

Healing
- Some can be healed with time
- Some require some bandaging so they don't get worse (and before they can heal)
- Some require magic of certain levels
- Some Antidotes
