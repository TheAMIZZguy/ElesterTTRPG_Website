NOTE TO SELF:

This is about all the different moments of a game the players might find themselves in

This page in "Playing the Game" is the overview of the different pillars of the game but then each one will have their own Folder




Combat
- Le fight

Exploration
- Travelling long distances
- "what is each character doing" mechanic where things can happen to them or issues occur/be resolved due to what they planned
- Large use of their skills when not in combat

Downtime
- Other free time that does not fit into any of the situations below
- generally free chatter moments and shopping
- getting to know things
- Can introduce mini-games and more casual events
- Crafting and skill-learning

Social "encounters" 
- more into encounters that require talking that are not the simple idle chat of talking to random NPCs
- It is more about talking to kings or other leader on getting something they need
- To guards to get past somewhere or not be arrested
- To get out of trouble of hostile enemies
- To get their plans in motion by convincing someone
- etc

Investigation
- Combing through rooms trying to find, learn, or loot 
- This is what will generally use the "points of interest" mechanic
- Combing through a room to find clues for a murder
- Finding useful items after they clear a dungeon room
- includes sneaking around
- etc 

 Operation
 - "Larger than the individual" moments
 - At the smaller level it is base-building and management, staff deals with maintainance
 - At the medium level it becomes autonomous base and recruitment of staff that is now interacting with the world and potentially guilds
 - At the larger level it is leading guilds
