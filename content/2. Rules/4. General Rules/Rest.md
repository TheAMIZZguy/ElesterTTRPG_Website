#### Rest
#Downtime #Rest
*Everyone must sleep, eventually.*

To gain the full benefits of rest at any tier.
 - Characters must sleep for a minimum of 6 uninterrupted hours
	- Up to two hours of Light Activity (usually keeping watch) may be included between the six hours, but do not count toward the hours of rest
		- *Example: Two hours of sleep, followed by two hours keeping watch, followed by four more hours of rest is sufficient for a full rest.*

If a character goes more than 20h without rest, they gain one level of Exhaustion
- If sleep is interrupted, this "timer" is paused for the hours they were asleep and resets only when rest has been completed

Taking long-term rest for faster recovery is part of downtime and can't be done during exploration.

#### Interrupted Rest
#Rest
*Rest can be interrupted by external factors such as combat, poor sleeping conditions, or an insufficient sleep cycle.*

Every time rest is interrupted, reduce the rest tier by one level
- This penalty can be reduced by sleeping an additional one hour. This does not increase the amount of light rest you can take between sleep

**Fighting during a Rest**:
- Character must succeed at a DC 15 flat check to avoid it getting counted as interrupted rest
	- If a character gains a level of Exhaustion or is downed during the fight, they automatically fail the roll

**Light Activity over 2h:**
- For every 30 minutes of light activity during a rest past 2h (rounding up), a character must succeed at a DC 5 flat check to avoid it getting counted as interrupted rest

**Moderate Activity:**
- For every 30 minutes of moderate activity during a rest (rounding up), a character must succeed at a DC 10 flat check to avoid it getting counted as interrupted rest

**Heavy Activity (not fighting):**
- For every 30 minutes of heavy activity during a rest (rounding up), a character must succeed at a DC 15 flat check to avoid it getting counted as interrupted rest


#### Restless
#Rest
*For those nights you can't sleep.*

- No HP recovery
- No Vitality recovery
- No MP recovery
- Exhaustion does not improve
- Gain the [[Tired]] condition if you are [[Rested]]
	- Roll a DC 10 flat check to avoid Exhaustion worsening by 1 level if you are already [[Tired]] or worse 
- Does not count as a full night's rest for any effects that depend on one


#### Wretched
#Rest 
*Sleeping on the bare ground without bedding. Sleeping with exposure to extreme weather, or unsanitary conditions.*

- No HP Recovery
- No Vitality recovery
- Recover 1 MP
- Exhaustion does not improve
- Gain the [[Tired]] condition unless protected from exposure

For 3 days in a row sleeping at Wretched or worse roll a DC 10 flat check, if you fail
- Your Exhaustion worsens by 1 level if you are already [[Tired]] or worse 
- This rest does not count as a full night's rest for any effects that depend on one


#### Poor
#Rest 
*Basic camp with bedding, moderate discomfort. Exposure to light elements.*

- 25% of Max HP recovered
- No Vitality recovery
- 25% of Max MP recovered
- Exhaustion remains unchanged

For 5 days in a row sleeping at Poor or worse 
- Roll a DC 10 flat check to avoid becoming [[Tired]]


#### Modest
#Rest 
*Proper camp with tent and bedding. Shared rooms in an inn. Safe environments.*

- 50% of Max HP recovered
- No Vitality recovery
- 25% of Max MP recovered
- Exhaustion remains unchanged

For every 3 days in a row sleeping at Modest or better 
- Roll a DC 10 flat check to reduce exhaustion by one tier 


#### Comfortable
#Rest 
*Well equipped campsite with amenities. Private room in an inn.*

- 75% of Max HP recovered
- No Vitality recovery
- 50% of Max MP recovered
- Exhaustion reduced by one tier of Exhaustion

For every 3 days in a row sleeping at Comfortable or better 
- Roll a DC 10 flat check to regain one point of Vitality


#### Luxurious
#Rest 
*Noble accommodations. Aetherically enhanced rest.*

- 100% of Max HP recovered
- Roll a DC 10 flat check to regain one point of Vitality
- 75% of Max MP recovered
- Exhaustion reduced by one tier of Exhaustion
