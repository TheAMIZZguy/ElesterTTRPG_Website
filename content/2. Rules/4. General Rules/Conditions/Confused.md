#### Confused
#Condition #Mental  
*You don't have your wits about you, and you attack wildly.*

- You are [[Off-Guard]] and you don't treat anyone as your ally
- You can't Delay, Ready, or use reactions
- You use all your actions to Strike or cast offensive cantrips
	- Though the GM can have you use other actions to facilitate attack, such as draw a weapon, move so target is in reach, and so forth
	- Your targets are determined randomly by the GM
	- If you have no viable targets, you target yourself, automatically hitting but you cannot crit
- If it's impossible for you to attack or cast spells, you babble incoherently, wasting your actions
- Each time you take damage from an attack or spell, you can attempt a DC 11 flat check to recover from your confusion and end the condition
