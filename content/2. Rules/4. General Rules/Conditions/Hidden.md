#### Hidden
#Condition #Detection 
*While you're hidden from a creature, that creature knows the space you're in but can't tell precisely where you are.* 

- A creature that you're hidden from is [[Off-Guard]] from you and must succeed at a DC 11 flat check when targeting you with an attack, spell, or other effect. 
	- If the check fails, you aren't affected. 
	- Area effects aren't subject to this flat check.
