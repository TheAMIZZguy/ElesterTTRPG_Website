#### Dumbfounded
#Condition #Mental
*You’re in a mental shock and cannot fully commit to maintaining a rational mind.*

- You take a –2 circumstance penalty to Mental AC
- Some effects give you the Dumbfounded condition only to certain creatures or against certain attacks. 
	- Others—especially conditions—can make you Dumbfounded against everything. 
	- If a rule doesn't specify that the condition applies only to certain circumstances, it applies to all of them, such as “The target is Dumbfounded”.
