#### Undetected
#Condition #Detection 
*While you're [[Undetected]] from a creature, that creature knows you're nearby but has no idea what space you occupy.* 

- A creature that you're Undetected from is [[Off-Guard]] from you and can guess which tile you're in to attempt an attack.
	- If you are not at the tile, your attack automatically fails
	- If you are at the tile, this works like targeting a [[Hidden]] creature (requiring a DC 11 flat check)
	- But the flat check and attack roll are rolled in secret by the GM, who doesn't reveal whether the attack missed due to failing the flat check, failing the attack roll, or choosing the wrong square.
		- Area effects aren't subject to this flat check.
