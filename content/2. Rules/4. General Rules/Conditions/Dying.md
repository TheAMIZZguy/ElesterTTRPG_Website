#### Dying
#Condition #Death
*You are desperately trying to hold on to life.* 

Dying occurs 
- When your character takes VP damage that leaves it at less than half its Max VP and you fall Unconscious instead of keep fighting
- When specific Poisons or Aetherics are ravaging your body

- Dying always includes a value, usually starting at a value equal to your current VP
	- Every hour you roll a DC 10 flat check
		- The first roll occurs when you become Dying
		- Every roll you fail, your VP decreases by 1
		- Every roll you pass, your Dying Value decreases by 1
