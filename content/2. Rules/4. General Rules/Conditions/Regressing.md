#### Regressing
#Condition #Aetherical  
*Your connection to the fields is reduced.*

- Regressing always includes a value
- You take a status penalty equal to the condition value on Presence-based checks and DCs, including AC and saves
- You also lose a number of Aether Points equal to your level (minimum 1) times the regressing value, and your maximum Aether Points are reduced by the same amount
	- For example, if you become Regressing 3 and you're a 3rd-level character, you lose 9 Aether Points and reduce your maximum Aether Points by 9
