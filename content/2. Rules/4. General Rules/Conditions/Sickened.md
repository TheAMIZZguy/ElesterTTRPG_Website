#### Sickened
#Condition #Physical #Tiered
*You feel ill.* 

- Sickened always includes a value
- You take a status penalty equal to this value on all your checks and DCs
- You can't willingly ingest anything—including essences and potions—while sickened.
- You can spend a single action retching in an attempt to recover
	- You immediately attempt a Stamina save against the DC of the effect that made you sickened
	- On a success, you reduce your sickened value by 1 (or by 2 on a critical success)
