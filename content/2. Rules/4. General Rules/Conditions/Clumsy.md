#### Clumsy
#Condition #Physical #Tiered
*Your movements become clumsy and inexact.*

- Clumsy always includes a value
- You take a status penalty equal to the condition value to Dexterity-based checks and DCs, including AC, saves, dexterity-based attack rolls, and skill checks using Agility, Slyness, Riding
