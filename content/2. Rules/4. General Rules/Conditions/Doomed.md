#### Doomed
#Condition #Death #Vitality #Tiered
*Your presence in the fields has been weakened, holding you closer to death.* 

- Doomed always includes a value 
	- Your maximum VP is reduced by your Doomed Value
	- If your maximum VP is reduced to 0, you instantly die.
- Your Doomed value decreases by 1 each time you get a full night's [[Rest]].
