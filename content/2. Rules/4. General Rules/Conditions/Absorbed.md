#### Absorbed
#Condition #Mental  
*You're compelled to focus your attention on something, distracting you from whatever else is going on around you.*

- You take a –2 status penalty to Perception and skill checks
- You can't use actions with the #concentrate trait unless they are related to the subject of your attention, as determined by the GM
- You take a –2 status penalty to any attack rolls, skill checks, and general actions that are not targeting to the subject of your attention, as determined by the GM
	- Such as attacking an enemy, healing an ally, or using Recall Knowledge on a creature that is not the target of your attention
	- An area of effect spell is not affected by this penalty if the target is included in the area
