#### Petrified
#Condition #Movement
*You have been turned to stone.* 

- You can't act nor sense anything
- You become an object with a Bulk double your normal Bulk, AC 9, Hardness 8 and the same HP as you had when alive, and have no [[Broken]] Threshold
- When the petrified condition ends, you have the same number of HP you had as a statue, and the same VP you had when petrified
- If the statue is destroyed, you immediately die
- While petrified, your mind and body are in stasis, so you don't age or notice the passing of time
