#### Encumbered
#Condition #Physical 
*You are carrying more weight than you can manage.*

- While you're encumbered, you're [[Clumsy|Clumsy 1]] and take a 2-unit penalty to all your Speeds
	- This can't reduce your Speed below 1 unit
