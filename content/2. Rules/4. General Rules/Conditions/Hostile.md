#### Hostile
#Condition #Relation
*A creature that is hostile to a character actively wants to harm that character.* 

- It will not accept Requests from that character, and may even accept requests against that character, given that it does not actively harm itself. 
	- Gain a -4 Status penalty to Persuasion checks with the creature
