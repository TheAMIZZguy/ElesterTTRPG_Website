#### Friendly
#Condition #Relation
*A creature that is friendly to a character likes that character.* 

- It will accept simple Requests from that character, as long as such requests aren't at the expense of the helpful creature's goals or quality of life. 
	- Gain a +2 Status bonus to Persuasion checks with the creature
