#### Invisible
#Condition #Sense
*You can't be seen.* 

- You're [[Undetected]] to everyone
- Creatures can Seek to detect you
	- if a creature succeeds at its Perception check against your Slyness DC, you become [[Hidden]] to that creature until you Sneak to become [[Undetected]] again
- If you become Invisible while someone can already see you, you start out [[Hidden]] to them (instead of [[Undetected]]) until you successfully Sneak. 
- You can't become [[Observed]] while invisible except via special abilities or magic.
