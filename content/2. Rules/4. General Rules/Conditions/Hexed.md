#### Hexed
#Condition #Aetherical #Malady #Tiered
*Your relation with the fields are tainted, disrupting your ability to recover and interact with magic.*

- Hexed always includes a value
- To avoid repetition, applicable purifications still work
	- Such as curing a simple TODO with a TODO even if you have Hexed 3
- If you are at Hexed 5 and you become no longer [[Dying]], you stay at Hexed 5 but do not regain the [[Dying]] condition unless something else causes it again

| Hexed Tier  | Effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hexed 1** | - Cannot recover VP from aetherical means<br>- [[Rest]] only regains half of the intended MP                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Hexed 2** | - Cannot recover VP from aetherical means<br>- [[Rest]] only regains half of the intended MP<br>- Regain only half of the intended HP from aetherical means<br>- Cannot remove conditions via non-aetherical means                                                                                                               |
| **Hexed 3** | - Cannot recover VP from aetherical means<br>- Cannot recover AP from [[Rest]]<br>- Cannot recover HP from aetherical means<br>- Cannot remove conditions via non-aetherical means                                                                                                                                               |
| **Hexed 4** | - Cannot recover VP from aetherical means<br>- Cannot recover MP from [[Rest]]<br>- Cannot recover HP from aetherical means<br>- Cannot remove conditions via non-aetherical means<br>- Your maximum AP is halved<br>- Gain the [[Suppressed]] condition                                                                         |
| **Hexed 5** | - Cannot recover VP from aetherical means<br>- Cannot recover MP from [[Rest]]<br>- Cannot recover HP from aetherical means<br>- Cannot remove conditions via non-aetherical means<br>- Your maximum MP is halved<br>- Gain the [[Suppressed]] condition<br>- Gain the [[Dying]] condition with a value equal to your current VP |

(Note to self when I come back to this when making spells and cures:)
- A heal (say "Mental Cure") would be able to heal any hex whose cause had the "Mental" trait, and only up to level 3
- A high level magical heal would be able to reduce any hexed condition by 1 (and the person would not be able to gain the benefits of this for a single poison more than once)
