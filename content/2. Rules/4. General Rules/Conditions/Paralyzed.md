#### Immobilized
#Condition #Movement
*You are frozen in place.* 

- You have the [[Off-Guard]] condition 
- You can't take actions except for Recall Knowledge and actions that require only your mind (determined by the GM)
- Your senses still function, but only in the areas you can perceive without moving (i.e. you can't Seek)
