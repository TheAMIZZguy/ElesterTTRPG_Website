To reduce the amount of conditions to learn, many of these can be treated as tiered
- **Senses**: Numbed (Sense) (Tier)
	- Blinded would be "Numbed Sight 3"
	- Overwhelmed Tremorsense would be "Numbed Tremorsense 1"
- **Detection**: Detected (Tier) 
	- Unnoticed would be "Detected 0"
	- Concealed would be "Detected 3"
- **Relation**: Relation (Tier)
	- Helpful would be "Relation 2"
	- Indifferent would be "Relation 0"
	- Unfriendly would be "Relation -1"
 - **Energy**: Exhausted (Tier) 
	- Rested would be "Exhausted 0"
	- Fatigued would be "Exhausted 3"
