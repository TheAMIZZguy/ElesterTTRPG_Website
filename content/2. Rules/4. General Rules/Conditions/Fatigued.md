#### Fatigued
#Condition #Rest
*You are struggling to summon much energy.* 

- -1 status penalty to all ability and skill checks 
- -1 status penalty to all attack rolls and ACs
- Movement speed reduced by 2 units
- You can't use exploration activities performed while traveling
- HP Maximum reduced by 25% (rounded down, to a minimum of 1)
