#### Overwhelmed
#Condition #Sense
*A sense is overstimulated.* 

- A given sense is reduced by one level of detection
	- [[Precise Sense]] is now at most Imprecise
	- [[Imprecise Sense]] is now at most Vague
	- [[Vague Sense]] is now at most Incapacitated
