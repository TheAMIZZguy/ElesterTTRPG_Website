#### Stupefied
#Condition #Mental #Tiered
*Your thoughts and instincts are clouded.*

- Stupefied always includes a value
- You take a status penalty equal to the condition value to Wits-based checks and DCs, including AC, saves, Wits-based attack rolls, and skill checks using Crafting, Perception, Aetherics, Tactics, and Survival
