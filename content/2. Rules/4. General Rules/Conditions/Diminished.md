#### Diminished
#Condition #Aetherical #Tiered
*Your readings of the fields are muted.*

- Diminished always includes a value
- You take a status penalty equal to the condition value to Charisma-based checks and DCs, including AC, saves, Charisma-based attack rolls, and skill checks using Persuasion, Aethermancy, Inspiration, Animal Handling, and Performance
