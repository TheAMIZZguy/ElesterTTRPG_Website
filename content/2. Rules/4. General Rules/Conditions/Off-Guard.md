#### Off-Guard
#Condition #Physical
*You're distracted or otherwise unable to focus your full attention on defense.*

- You take a –2 circumstance penalty to Physical AC
- Some effects give you the Off-Guard condition only to certain creatures or against certain attacks. 
	- Others—especially conditions—can make you Off-Guard against everything. 
	- If a rule doesn't specify that the condition applies only to certain circumstances, it applies to all of them, such as “The target is Off-Guard”.
