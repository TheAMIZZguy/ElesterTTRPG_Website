#### Enfeebled
#Condition #Physical #Tiered
*You're physically weakened.*

- Enfeebled always includes a value
- You take a status penalty equal to your enfeebled value on Strength-based checks and DCs, including AC, saves, strength-based melee attack rolls, and skill checks using Fortitude
