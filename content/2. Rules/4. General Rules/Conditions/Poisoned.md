#### Poisoned
#Condition #Poison #Malady #Tiered
*Your body is battling a powerful toxin, impairing its ability to recover.*

- Poisoned always includes a value
- To avoid repetition, applicable antidotes still work
	- Such as curing a simple TODO with a TODO even if you have Poisoned 3
- If you are at Poisoned 5 and you become no longer [[Dying]], you stay at Poisoned 5 but do not regain the [[Dying]] condition unless something else causes it again

| Poisoned Tier  | Effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Poisoned 1** | - Cannot recover VP from non-aetherical means other than [[Rest]]<br>- [[Rest]] does not recover HP                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Poisoned 2** | - Cannot recover VP from non-aetherical means<br>- [[Rest]] does not recover HP<br>- Conditions cannot be removed via non-aetherical means                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Poisoned 3** | - Cannot recover VP from non-aetherical means<br>- Cannot recover HP from non-aetherical means<br>- Cannot remove conditions via non-aetherical means<br>- [[Rest]] is treated as one tier lower                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Poisoned 4** | - Cannot recover VP from non-aetherical means<br>- Cannot recover HP from non-aetherical means<br>- Cannot remove conditions via non-aetherical means<br>- [[Rest]] is treated as one tier lower<br>- Gain the [[Sickened]] condition<br>- Gain the [[Tired]] condition if you do not already have it<br>- Gain the [[2. Rules/4. General Rules/Conditions/Slowed]] 1 Condition                                                                     |
| **Poisoned 5** | - Cannot recover VP from non-aetherical means<br>- Cannot recover HP from non-aetherical means<br>- Cannot remove conditions via non-aetherical means<br>- [[Rest]] is treated as one tier lower<br>- Gain the [[Sickened]] condition<br>- Gain the [[Tired]] condition if you do not already have it<br>- Gain the [[2. Rules/4. General Rules/Conditions/Slowed]] 1 Condition<br>- Gain the [[Dying]] condition with a value equal to your current VP |

(Note to self when I come back to this when making spells and cures:)
- A heal (say "Spore Cure") would be able to heal any poison whose cause had the "Spore" trait, and only up to level 3
- A high level magical heal would be able to reduce any poisoned condition by 1 (and the person would not be able to gain the benefits of this for a single poison more than once)
