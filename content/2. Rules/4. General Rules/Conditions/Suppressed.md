#### Suppressed
#Condition #Aetherical
*Your focus in the fields shimmers, leaving you vulnerable.*

- You take a –2 circumstance penalty to Aetherical AC
- Some effects give you the Suppressed condition only to certain creatures or against certain attacks. 
	- Others—especially conditions—can make you Suppressed against everything. 
	- If a rule doesn't specify that the condition applies only to certain circumstances, it applies to all of them, such as “The target is Suppressed”.
