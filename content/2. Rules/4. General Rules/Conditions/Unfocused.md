#### Unfocused
#Condition #Mental #Tiered
*Your mind is having trouble concentrating on the situation at hand.*

- Unfocused always includes a value
- You take a status penalty equal to the condition value on Willpower-based checks and DCs, including AC and saves
- When making a reaction, you must pass a Will save with a DC equal to the unfocused (minimum 1) value times three
	- If you fail the save, the resources and action points are still used up but the effects of the reaction do not occur
