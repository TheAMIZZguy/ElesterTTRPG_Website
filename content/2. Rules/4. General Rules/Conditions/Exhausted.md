#### Exhausted
#Condition #Rest
*You feel like collapsing at any moment.* 

- -2 status penalty to all ability and skill checks 
- -2 status penalty to all attack rolls and ACs
- Movement speed reduced by 2 units
- You can't use exploration activities performed while traveling
- HP Maximum reduced by 50% (rounded up)
