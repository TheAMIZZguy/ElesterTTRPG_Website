#### Immobilized
#Condition #Movement
*You are incapable of movement.* 

- You can't use any actions that have the move trait
- If you're immobilized by something holding you in place
- If an external force would move you out of your space, the force must succeed at a check against either the DC of the effect holding you in place or the relevant defense (usually Physical Defense) of the creature holding you in place.
