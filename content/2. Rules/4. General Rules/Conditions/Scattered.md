#### Scattered
#Condition #Mental #Tiered
*Your thoughts are all over the place and have difficulty recalling information.*

- Scattered always includes a value
- You take a status penalty equal to the condition value to Knowledge-based checks and DCs, including AC, saves, Knowledge-based attack rolls, and skill checks using Engineering, Aetherology, History, and Nature
