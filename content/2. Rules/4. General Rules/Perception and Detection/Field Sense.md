#### Field Sense
#Sense 
*Sense with the fields not with the physical senses.* 

- Counts as a Precise Sense when there is little ambiguity
	- A fire in a cold area
- Counts as an Imprecise Sense when there is some ambiguity
	- A living creature in a garden
- Counts as a Vague Sense when there is a lot of ambiguity
	- Distinguishing a specific mineral from a pile of rocks

A field can be blocked
- Black iron blocks all vision past it
- A wall of stone will block Field Sense (Existence) beyond it
- Seeing heat through a wall of ice (even if transparent and visible with eyes)
