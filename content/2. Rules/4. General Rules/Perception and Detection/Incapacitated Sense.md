#### Incapacitated Sense
#Sense 
*A sense that is not able to be used* 

- The sense cannot be used as a Precise, Imprecise, nor Vague sense
- If you only use an Incapacitated sense then every creature is Unnoticed to you
- You cannot detect anything using this sense
	- You automatically critically fail Perception checks that require you to use this sense
- You are immune to effects that would effect this sense
