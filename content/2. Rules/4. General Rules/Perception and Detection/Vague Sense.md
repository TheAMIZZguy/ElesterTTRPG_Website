#### Vague Sense
#Sense 
*A sense that can alert you that something is there but aren't useful for zeroing in on it to determine exactly what it is.* 

- You can usually sense a creature automatically with an imprecise sense 
	- But it has the Undetected condition instead of the Observed, Concealed, or Hidden conditions
- You take a -4 Status penalty to Perception checks that use this sense

Average smell is a vague sense.
