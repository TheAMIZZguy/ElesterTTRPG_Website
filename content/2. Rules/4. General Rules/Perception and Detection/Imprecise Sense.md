#### Imprecise Sense
#Sense 
*A sense that can’t detect the full range of detail that a precise sense can.* 

- You can usually sense a creature automatically with an imprecise sense 
	- But it has the Hidden condition instead of the Observed or Concealed condition
- It might be Undetected by you if it’s using Stealth or is in an environment that distorts the sense, such as a noisy room in the case of hearing.
	- In which case you can use the Seek basic action to better detect the creature.
- You take a -2 Status penalty to Perception checks that use this sense

Average hearing is an imprecise sense. Touch is an imprecise sense, but can only be used for things you are touching.
