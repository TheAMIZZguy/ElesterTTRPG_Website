Countless species and scholars have sought to understand the permeating forces of the world and have come up with semi-unified theological and philosophical understanding. To many who can see the fields, it is about consciousness


## The Fields
The fundamental building block and backplate of the universe has been found to be that of the fields. Any object, person, or idea can be deconstructed into a combination of the fields in either positive or negative excitations. 
Neither side is good or evil, but rather opposite sides of the same coin.


### Thought
The field that governs conscious thought as we know it; all living beings, flora or fauna. If it thinks in the ways that we think, then it is doused in the field of thought.
Thought is the field of the animate, of will and awareness.

#### Life
Growth and free will. 
Much beloved by many for its calming and healing properties. It has also been used to imbue objects with sentience and to enhance one's mind.

#### Death
Stillness and instinct.
Hated by some for it's abilities to take away from others, it has been used extensively to study the aetherical properties of objects


### Existence
The field that governs where things are; it dictates the interactions of matters and its transformations. If it can be touched, then your are feeling this field.
Existence is the field of the inanimate, of matter and its negation. 

#### Earth
Matter and its properties.
Transmutation and creation is determined by earth.

#### Blight
Void trying to reclaim the place of matter. 
Poison and destruction is determined by blight.


### Force
The field that governs why things happen in the ways the do; from what we can see to the way water falls. If it moves then it is following the will of force.
Force is the field of interaction, of push and pull. 

#### Primordial
That of active interaction.
All the movement you see in the world around you is an aspect of the primordial. 

#### Attuned
That of selective interaction, forces that interact with themselves.
It is the tingle of electricity or the light off a chandelier. All the light you see and the force of magnetism is attuned.
 

### Energy
The field that governs the fields itself; the limits of the power and undoing. Any fire, chill, and craze of the elements is the field of energy.
Energy is the field of intensity, of spark and sink.

#### Fire
That of increasing power, the fields themselves increasing in concentration.
All the accumulation of fields is fire and often lets out heat to normalize

#### Ice
That of decreasing power, the spreading of the fields.
Degradation of the fields is ice and often lets out heat to normalize


## Maxems
The wonder workers of the humans. Anyone who is able to influence the fields is called a maxem. 
Humans are a unique species out of all of the ones that have domain over the aether. 
Unique because this dominion is not available to every human.
In addition to the rare case that different maxems have access to different elements.


## Vasilians 
Diplomats, beast masters, master traders; vasilians are known by many different names.
Possibly the most unique ability unique to some humans is that of reading and sensing the fields.
Rarer still because vasilians do not necessarily have control over the fields they can sense.
Someone who is both a maxem and a vasilian is called a magus and may have control over every field. 


## Beasts
Species of animals (and some other beings like treants) that can control the fields are known as Beasts.
Unlike humans, beasts are not necessarily limited by a single element.
There are several beast species that are quite intelligent and have their own societies.
Many of these beast civilizations have a complicated history of war, peace, and trade with humans. 


## Blooms
Analogous to beasts, blooms are plants life that influence the fields.
While not necessarily limited by a single element, they are generally more passive and reactive in their use. 
Many of these plants are highly coveted as ingredients for creating essences, leading to a lot of deaths in those that are unprepared. 


## Crystals
Just as the fields are everywhere, even simple matter may be imbued with influence of the fields.
Though available in all forms of matter, the most famous is that of solid stones purifying themselves with the aether to become gemstones.
These gemstones are often collected for their beauty for beauty or power stored within, and are central to many ceremonies.
Flowing streams of gaseous and liquid crystals often leave chaos in their wake; disrupting the field and causing unpredictable power surges.
